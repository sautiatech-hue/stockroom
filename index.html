Stockroom

A simple inventory manager for keeping track of the products that matter to your shop.

Record products, quantities, categories, prices, expiry dates, and photos. Search and filter your catalogue, review stock warnings, and import or export product lists as CSV.

Your inventory is stored on your device. You can optionally back it up to your Google Drive account; Stockroom creates a Stockroom Backups folder for its backup files.
