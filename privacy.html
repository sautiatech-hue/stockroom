Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains how Stockroom handles information when you use the inventory app.

Information stored on your device

Products, categories, prices, quantities, notes, tags, dates, history, and product photos that you enter are stored locally on your device. Stockroom does not operate a server that collects this inventory data, and the app does not use advertising or analytics services.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to back up or restore your inventory, Stockroom uses Google Sign-In and the Google Drive API. A backup can contain your inventory records, categories, history, and product photos. Backups are stored in the Stockroom Backups folder in your Google Drive account.

Stockroom requests the drive.file permission, which is limited to Drive files created or opened by this app. It does not access unrelated files in your Drive. Backup data is sent to Google Drive at your request and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. Stockroom does not send your inventory to a server operated by the app's developer.

Exports and deletion

If you export a CSV, the file is saved on your device. You control where you keep or share that file. You can remove local app data using the app or your device settings, and you can delete backup files from Google Drive. Deleting the app does not necessarily delete backups already stored in Drive.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. Questions about this policy can be raised through the repository where Stockroom is published.
